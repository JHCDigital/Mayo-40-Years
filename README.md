# Introducing the Speaker
## Virginia Wright-Peterson
Virginia Wright-Peterson is an author and educator with a special interest in untold stories, especially women's history and the history of the Mayo Clinic. She worked in Mayo Clinic administration in Jacksonville, Florida when the campus opened in 1986. Wright-Peterson also served as a Fullbright Scholar in Algeria and worked with the American Red Cross on a U.S. military base in Iraq. Her three previous books, including "Women of Mayo Clinic: The Founding Generation, were finalists for her home state Book Award in Minnesota. 
 ## Dr. James Bolling
 Dr. James Bolling is a graduate of the University of Kentucky College of Medicine. After training in Ophthalmology, Retinal diseases and intraocular tumors, Dr. Bolling joined the staff of Mayo Clinic in Rochester Minnesota in 1986 and shortly after joined the staff of Mayo Clinic Florida as one of the original physicians at Mayo Florida. Dr. Bolling also worked for 30 years as a consultant at Nemours Children's Clinic. He served as chair of the department of Ophthalmology and for 8 years on the Mayo Clinic Florida Board of Governors. Dr. Bolling retired from clinical practice in 2021 and is currently a senior research fellow at Mayo Clinic where he assists with research and administrative activities on a part-time basis when not visiting grandchildren or playing the banjo.
