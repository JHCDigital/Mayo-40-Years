# Introducing the Speaker
## Virginia Wright-Peterson
Virginia Wright-Peterson is a Minnesotan author, educator and speaker. Her books explore themes of intersectionality, history and resilience, with a special focus on women's history and the history of Mayo Clinic. This program celebrated the publication of her fourth book, "Mayo Clinic in Florida: A Legacy of Dedication, Innovation, and Excellence," which was released in tandem with Mayo Clinic's 40th anniversary in Florida. Her three previous books were all finalists for the Minnesota Book Award. Wright-Peterson has been teaching for over fifteen years at schools like the University of Minnesota Rochester and Rochester Community and Technical College. She was also a Fullbright Scholar who taught at the University of Algeria. Additionally, she worked alongside the American Red Cross on a U.S. Military base in Iraq. Wright-Peterson continues to support and engage with the history and writing communities through attending events and offering consultations.
